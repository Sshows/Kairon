## OWASP Cheat Sheet Series

The files in `content/web-originals/cheat-sheets/` are a complete snapshot of
the 137 Markdown files in the upstream `cheatsheets/` directory at commit
`920293be317721982fa50324a6f26af3f29dc45e` of
https://github.com/OWASP/CheatSheetSeries .

Copyright belongs to the OWASP Cheat Sheet Series authors and contributors.
The project is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . The full
upstream license is retained as `content/web-originals/cheat-sheets/LICENSE.md`.
Article-specific attributions, notices, and licenses remain inside each file,
including the CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution in the Infrastructure as Code article.

The Markdown source files are preserved without translating or abridging their
text. Kairon's reader changes presentation and resolves relative hyperlinks;
raw HTML is not executed. Article pages identify the authors, pinned source,
license, and these presentation changes. Kairon's Russian introductions and
original exercises are separately authored and labeled as such. No endorsement
by OWASP is implied.

## OWASP Juice Shop

Juice Shop is authored by Bjoern Kimminich and the OWASP Juice Shop contributors
and released under MIT. The course targets v20.3.0, commit
`f189f521266f42c6bd05884468292000cee852ed`:
https://github.com/juice-shop/juice-shop .
Kairon links to the official application and explains how learners run their
own local copy. The intentionally vulnerable application is not hosted by
Kairon. Any retained challenge metadata is identified as upstream MIT content.
Kairon's lesson text and exercises are original. The companion guide is linked,
not copied or translated; its separate CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 license is respected.

## Hacker101

Hacker101 is a free course by HackerOne and its contributors:
https://github.com/Hacker0x01/hacker101 . The audited source is licensed under
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Kairon provides independently authored topic introductions
and links to the official lessons and videos. Videos, transcripts, slides,
and course texts are not mirrored. No affiliation is implied.
